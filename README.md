# Promptpanel

**Your AI prompts, one hotkey away.** Local, private, fast. Windows only.

Promptpanel keeps your best AI prompts one keypress away. Store your library
locally, trigger any prompt with a hotkey, and paste it straight into Claude,
ChatGPT, Copilot, or anywhere you type. No cloud account, no tracking - your
prompts stay on your machine.

## Editions

- **Lite (free)** - Full prompt library, 3 hotkey slots, English UI,
  screenshot snip. A single AutoHotkey-based executable, no installer.
- **Pro ($19 one-time)** - 9 hotkey slots, screenshot annotation, usage
  analytics, backups, prompt templates, and automatic updates. Launch price is
  $15 for the first 72 hours. One-time purchase, no subscription. Pro comes with
  a free trial, and every Pro purchase is covered by a 14-day money-back
  guarantee.

## Download

Grab the latest build from the
[Releases](https://github.com/kewkew89/promptpanel/releases/latest) page:

- `Promptpanel-Lite-<version>.zip` - Lite edition, free, no installer (Windows)
- `Promptpanel-Setup-<version>.exe` - Pro installer with free trial (Windows)

### SmartScreen notice

Promptpanel is a new Windows app, so SmartScreen may show a "Windows protected
your PC" warning the first time you run it. Click **More info**, then **Run
anyway** to continue. This warning fades as more people install the app.
