# Promptpanel

**Your AI prompts, one hotkey away.** Local, private, fast.

Website: [https://promptpanel.app](https://promptpanel.app)

Promptpanel keeps your best AI prompts one keypress away. Store your library
locally, trigger any prompt with a hotkey, and paste it straight into Claude,
ChatGPT, Copilot, or anywhere you type. No cloud account, no tracking - your
prompts stay on your machine.

## Editions

- **Lite (free)** - Full prompt library, 3 hotkey slots, English/Swedish UI,
  screenshot snip. A single AutoHotkey-based executable, no installer.
- **Pro ($19 one-time)** - 9 hotkey slots, screenshot annotation, usage
  analytics, backups, prompt templates, and automatic updates.

## Download

Grab the latest build from the [Releases](https://github.com/kewkew89/promptpanel/releases/latest) page:

- `Promptpanel-Setup-<version>.exe` - Pro installer (Windows)
- `Promptpanel-Lite-<version>.zip` - Lite edition (Windows)
